## Notes on Patterns

### Fingers and Toes
- Pattern: **[hand/foot] + ci** = finger/toe
- **Shiu ci** = finger (hand + digit)
- **Kiok ci** = toe (foot + digit)

### Thumb and Pinky
- **Kung** = big/main (for thumb and big toe)
- **Mui** = little/small (for pinky and little toe)

### Nails
- **Kap** = nail/claw
- **Shiu ci kap** = fingernail
