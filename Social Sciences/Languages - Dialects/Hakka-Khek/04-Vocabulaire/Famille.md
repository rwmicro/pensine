## Singkawang [SKW]
Kinship terms and loanwords, with Chinese characters and Pha̍k-fa-sṳ romanization:

| Hakka (characters, Pha̍k-fa-sṳ) | English                                  |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 阿狗 a-kièu (*akeo*)              | term for a boy (lit. "the dog")                  |
| 阿哥 a-ko (*ako*)                 | older brother                                    |
| 阿妹 a-mói (*amoi*)               | younger sister / girl                            |
| 阿伯 a-pak (*apak*)               | uncle (father's older brother)                   |
| 阿叔 a-shuk (*asuk*)              | uncle (father's younger brother)                 |
| 姊姊 tsê-tsê (*cece*)             | older sister                                     |
| 公公 kung-kung (*kungkung*)       | **paternal** grandfather                         |
| 婆婆 phô-phô (*popo*)             | **maternal** grandmother                         |
Note: unlike Pontianak (akung/apho, with no distinction by side of the family), Singkawang Hakka explicitly distinguishes the paternal grandfather from the maternal grandmother.
