## Common Patterns

### Asking "Have you...?"
- **[Verb] + pau + mang?** = Have you [verb] yet?
- Example: **Sit pau mang?** = Have you eaten?

### Making Questions
- **[Statement] + mo?** = [Statement]?
- Example: **Nyi ho mo?** = Are you well?

### Saying "For you"
- **[Verb] + pun nyi** = [Verb] for you
- Example: **Mai pun nyi** = Buy for you

### Expressing "Very"
- **An + [adjective]** = very [adjective]
- Example: **An ho** = very good

## Examples in Context

1. **Ngai nyi phenjiu café e bi**
   - I met my friend at the café

2. **Li ha ngai buk kha**
   - Right now I'm at home

3. **Mak kai tu jiu**
   - Everything is available

4. **Ten jit ha**
   - Wait a minute

5. **Nyi nai bui?**
   - Where are you?

## Appendix [PTK]

| Hakka      | Likely meaning                                                                  | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **hiau**   | can, to know how to (capability auxiliary)                                     | Appears in almost all "Bisakah/Dapatkah...?" ("Can you...?") questions                                                                                                                                                |
| **khiun**  | to be/be located (somewhere), close to "ada"/"berada"                          | "lift khiun un sak" = the elevator is over there                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **liak**   | this/that (demonstrative)                                                      | "liak chiu" = this arm; "liak bie" = here (lit. "this place")                                                                                                                                                         |
| **bie**    | place, location                                                                | Mostly in compounds: liak bie = here, khiun...bie = there                                                                                                                                                             |
| **kien**   | room — also a component meaning "duration/moment"                             | Close to Chinese 間 (jiān), used for both rooms and time (時間)                                                                                                                                                         |
| **nyia**   | your (possessive form of nyi)                                                  | "nyia senhicha" = your reservation                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **nyip**   | to enter                                                                       | "hi nyip hilton" = to enter the Hilton hotel; "nyip nyia pin" = enter your PIN code                                                                                                                                   |
| **tungsi** | thing, stuff                                                                   | Consistent with the Cantonese/Hakka compound 東西 (tung-si, lit. "east-west") already known with this meaning. The variant "tung si" (with a space) should not be confused with "thung si" = perfume, a near-homophone with a different meaning |
| **siw**    | to help / to give (exact meaning uncertain)                                    | Always after "then" (tolong): "then siw ngai" = help me / give me                                                                                                                                                     |
| **ti**     | to know                                                                        | "kong ngai ti [question]" = "tell me..."                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **met**    | to do, undertake (project auxiliary: "to be in the process of / plan to")      | "Ngai teu met ten pesta" = we're preparing a party                                                                                                                                                                    |
