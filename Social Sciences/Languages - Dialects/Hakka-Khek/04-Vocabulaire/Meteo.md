# Weather and Temperature

## Basic Weather Conversation

```
A: Kinyit chit fun sau eee (Today is very hot)
B: He wa sau to emo kong (Yes, so hot, I have no words to explain)
A: Ngai chi to si boi lok sui ee, thet sau ko theu
   (I think it will rain, because it's too hot)
B: He wa biasa si he thet sau kia tanda boi lok sui
   (Yes, sometimes when it's too hot it means it'll rain)
A: Pok ki lok sui, mo pun si thet sau
   (Hope it rains, can't stand if it's too hot)
B: Nyi khon ngai chut hon (See, I'm sweating)
```

| English           | Hakka         |

## Grammar Patterns

### Expressing Opinions About Weather
- **Ngai chi to...** = I think/feel...
- **Si boi...** = will probably...
- **Thet...ko theu** = because it's too...

### Expressing Consequences
- **Tanda** = sign/indication
- **Kia tanda boi...** = it's a sign that will...

### Expressing Hope
- **Pok ki** = hope that
- **Mo pun si** = can't stand if

### Making Observations
- **Nyi khon** = look/see
- **He wa** = yes, that's right
- **Biasa si** = usually when

## Useful Expressions

- **Chit fun** = very (intensifier for conditions)
- **Emo kong** = no words (to explain)
- **Ko theu** = because of that
- **He wa** = yes/that's right (agreement)
- **Biasa** = usually
