# Transportation and Clothing

## Transportation

| English       | Hakka     | Notes              |
|---------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Walking       | hang lu   | Literally "walk road" |
| Car           | hi cha    |                   |
| Train         | fo cha    | Literally "fire car" |
| Bike          | kiok cha  | Literally "foot car" |
| Plane         | fui ki    |                   |

## Notes

- **Cha** (車) = vehicle/car
- Many transportation words use **cha** as a suffix
- **Hang lu** = walk (literally "walk road")
