## Common Food Phrases

| English                           | Hakka                         |
|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Have you eaten?                   | Sit pau mang?                 |
| I've already eaten                | Ngai sit pau                  |
| I haven't eaten yet               | Ngai mang sit                 |
| What did you eat?                 | Sit ma'ai?                    |
| I ate rice with chicken and vegetables | Ngai sit fan pong kai nyuk kak choi |
| I ate pasta with beef             | Ngai sit pastas kak nyiu nyuk |
| Is it good?                       | Boi ho sit mo?                |
| Very delicious                    | An ho sit                     |

## Example Sentences

1. **Ngai sit fan pong kai nyuk kak choi**
   - I ate rice with chicken and vegetables

2. **Sit ho kopi mang?**
   - Have you had coffee?

3. **Ngai an nyen sit Nasi Goreng**
   - I really like eating Nasi Goreng

4. **Sit lang-sui**
   - Drink cold water

5. **Li kian an ho sit**
   - This is very delicious

## Notes

- **Sit** is used for both eating and drinking
- **Kak** = with (to connect foods: fan kak choi = rice with vegetables)
- **Pong** = with/and (connector)
- Many Indonesian/Malay food words are borrowed (bakso, Nasi Goreng, etc.)
