- *Bahasa Khek* (Scribd 737172533) and *Kamus Sehari-hari Bahasa Hakka/Khek* (Scribd 817384488) - Two community word lists in Indonesian, compiled from social-media posts (2023-2024). No author, no tones, variety not stated; entries taken from them are marked [COM]

## Academic Sources

More rigorous than the blogs above, used to distinguish Pontianak Hakka from Singkawang Hakka:
- Huang, Su-chen (2013). *印尼坤甸客家話研究* [Pontianak Hakka Study]. Master's thesis, National Central University (Taiwan)
- Huang, Hui-chen (2008). *印尼山口洋客家話研究* [Study of Hakka in Singkawang, Indonesia]. Master's thesis, National Central University (Taiwan)
- Pontoh, Manzhuur Daanisy Ahmad; Kabul, Assa Rahmawati (2023). "Kata Serapan dari Bahasa Hakka dalam Leksikon Bahasa Indonesia". *Semiotika*, 24(1), 118–132, Universitas Indonesia. Vocabulary verified with two native Singkawang Hakka speakers.
- Sujaini, Herry (2020). "Improving the role of language model in statistical machine translation (Indonesian-Javanese)". *International Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering*, 10(2), 2102. Reference paper of the [Korpus Nusantara](https://github.com/herrysujaini/korpusnusantara) (Universitas Tanjungpura), Indonesian sentences translated into 25 regional languages by Informatics students. Its "Khek Pontianak" sheet (1,986 sentences, mostly travel phrases) is the source of the [[Social Sciences/Languages - Dialects/Hakka-Khek/02-Communication/Voyage|travel note]] and of the variant counts in [[Social Sciences/Languages - Dialects/Hakka-Khek/05-Culture/Dialectes-Regionaux|Regional Dialects]]. Free for research use with citation; translation quality is uneven (Indonesian words left untranslated, inconsistent spelling, no tones)
