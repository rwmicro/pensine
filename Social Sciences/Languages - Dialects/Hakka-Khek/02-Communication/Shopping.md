# Shopping and Buying Things

## Buying a Book at a Store

```
Customer: Ngai siong mai jit pun su
          (I want to buy a book)

Seller:   Oi mai ma'ai su?
          (What book do you want to buy?)

Customer: Jit pun siau sot
          (A novel)

Seller:   Li kian su tiam an thai, mak kai tu jiu
          (This bookstore is very big, everything is available)

Customer: Ho…ten jit ha ngai chim pun nyi.. nyi to, nyi to
          (Okay, wait a minute I'll search for you… I found it, I found it)

Seller:   Thang kong li pun su an jiu ji si
          (People say this book is very interesting)

Customer: He wa nyi siong oi mai mo?
          (Yes, do you want to buy it?)

Buyer:    Ngai oi mai wa, jit to lui?
          (I want it, how much?)

Seller:   Pak nyi ng chien (125k)

Buyer:    Ho ngai oi, lia ngai phoi nyi pak nyi ng chien
          (Yes I want it, here I give you 125k)

Seller:   Kamsia (Thank you)
```

## Key Phrases

### Expressing Want/Need
- **Ngai siong mai...** = I want to buy...
- **Oi mai ma'ai?** = What do you want to buy?
- **Ngai oi mai wa** = I want to buy (it)
- **Nyi siong oi mai mo?** = Do you want to buy (it)?

### Asking About Price
- **Jit to lui?** = How much is it?
- **Pak nyi ng chien** = 125 thousand (125,000)

### Completing Purchase
- **Lia ngai phoi nyi...** = Here, I give you...
- **Ho ngai oi** = Yes, I want it
- **Kamsia** = Thank you

## Numbers for Shopping

Common price expressions:
- **Jit chien** = 1,000
- **Nyi sip chien** = 20,000
- **Pak chien** = 100,000
- **Pak nyi ng chien** = 125,000

## Other Shopping Contexts

### General Buying
- **Mai** = buy
- **Bon** = pay (for services)
- **Tiam** = store/shop

### Locations
- **Su tiam** = bookstore
- **Lusun** = roadside (market)

## Example Sentences

1. **Ngai siong mai jit pun su**
   - I want to buy a book

2. **Li kian su tiam an thai**
   - This bookstore is very big

3. **Jit to lui?**
   - How much is it?

4. **Ngai phoi nyi pak nyi ng chien**
   - I give you 125,000

## Notes

- **Siong** can mean "think," "want," or "remember" depending on context
- **Tiam** can mean "store," "time," or "quiet" - context is key
- **Pun nyi** = for you (when doing something for someone)
