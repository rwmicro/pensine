# Making Plans and Appointments

## Planning to Watch a Movie

```
Person A: Kinyit oi hi khon hi mo?
          (Do you want to watch a movie today?)

Person B: Ho..kit to tiam?
          (Sure, what time?)

Person A: Sam tiam
          (3 pm)

Person B: Oi nai bui nyi?
          (Where will we meet?)

Person A: Bioskop mian chian bi ok?
          (In front of the cinema, okay?)

Person B: Ok ngai kai bui ten nyi ha sam tiam
          (Okay, I'll wait for you there at 3)
```

## Meeting People

### Talking About Meeting Friends
```
Kinyit ngai chi to an ho, ngai nyi ngai phenjiu café e bi
(Today I'm feeling good, I met my friend at the café)
```

### Key Meeting Phrases
- **Nyi** = meet
- **Phenjiu** = friend
- **Siong nyi** = meet (formal)
- **Nyi ngai phenjiu** = meet my friend
- **Kai bui** = wait
- **Ten nyi** = for you

## Making Suggestions

### Proposing Activities
- **Oi...mo?** = Do you want to...?
- **Hi...** = go to...
- **Khon...** = watch/see...

### Examples:
1. **Kinyit oi hi khon hi mo?**
   - Do you want to watch a movie today?

2. **Oi nai bui nyi?**
   - Where shall we meet?

3. **Bioskop mian chian bi ok?**
   - In front of the cinema, okay?

## Responding to Invitations

### Accepting
- **Ho** = Okay/Sure
- **Ok** = Okay
- **Ngai oi** = I want to

### Asking for Details
- **Kit to tiam?** = What time?
- **Nai bui?** = Where?
- **Oi nai bui nyi?** = Where will we meet?

## Complete Example Dialogue

```
A: Kinyit oi hi khon hi mo?
   (Want to watch a movie today?)

B: Ho! Kit to tiam?
   (Sure! What time?)

A: Sam tiam. Oi nai bui nyi?
   (3 pm. Where shall we meet?)

B: Bioskop mian chian bi ok?
   (In front of the cinema okay?)

A: Ok, ngai kai bui ten nyi ha sam tiam
   (Okay, I'll wait for you there at 3)
```

## Useful Phrases

- **Ngai kai bui ten nyi ha...** = I'll wait for you at...
- **...bi ok?** = ...okay? (seeking agreement)
- **Oi nai bui nyi?** = Where will we meet?
- **Hi khon hi** = go watch a movie

## Notes

- **Hi** can mean both "go" and "movie" depending on context
- **Khon** = watch/see/look
- **Nyi** as a verb = meet (different from "nyi" = you)
- **Bui** = wait/meet (location context)
