# Formal vs Informal Indonesian

Indonesian has distinct formal and informal registers. **Informal** is used most of the time with friends, family, and peers. **Formal** is mandatory when speaking to strangers, older people, or in professional/official settings.

## When to Use Each Register

### Informal (Casual)
- With friends and family
- With people your age or younger
- In casual conversations
- On social media

### Formal (Polite)
- With strangers
- With older people (elders)
- In professional settings
- In official situations
- When showing respect

## Examples in Context

### Greeting Someone
**Informal:** *Hai, kamu siapa?* = "Hi, who are you?"
**Formal:** *Selamat pagi, siapa nama Anda?* = "Good morning, what is your name?"

### Asking for Help
**Informal:** *Bisa bantu aku gak?* = "Can you help me?"
**Formal:** *Bisakah Anda membantu saya?* = "Could you help me?"

### Saying Goodbye
**Informal:** *Yaudah, bye!* = "Alright, bye!"
**Formal:** *Baiklah, selamat tinggal* = "Well then, goodbye"
