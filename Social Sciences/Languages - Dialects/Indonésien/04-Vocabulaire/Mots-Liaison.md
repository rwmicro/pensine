## Usage Examples

### Temporal Examples
- **Sebelum makan, cuci tangan** = Before eating, wash your hands
- **Sesudah kerja, saya mau istirahat** = After work, I want to rest
- **Sambil makan, dia nonton TV** = While eating, he/she watches TV

### Logical Examples
- **Saya capek karena kerja banyak** = I'm tired because of a lot of work
- **Hujan, jadi saya tidak keluar** = It's raining, so I'm not going out
- **Walaupun mahal, saya beli** = Although it's expensive, I bought it

### Purpose Examples
- **Saya belajar untuk ujian** = I study for the exam
- **Dia datang supaya bisa bantu** = He/she came in order to help
